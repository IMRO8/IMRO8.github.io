Privacy and ML security research01

Privacy & Utility Benchmarking of Tabular Diffusion Models

Built a controlled benchmark comparing TabDDPM, CDTD, and TabSyn with PrivBayes across six mixed, categorical, and numerical datasets. The evaluation covered classification and regression utility, feature-level fidelity, pairwise correlation, coverage, and generalization using 10 synthetic datasets per configuration with 95% confidence intervals. The comparison was also extended to DPWGAN on real-data benchmarks.

  • Used KDE over PCA projections to expose mode collapse and coverage failures that downstream task metrics did not reveal.
  • Showed that an off-the-shelf DP-SGD adaptation reduced balanced accuracy from 0.77–0.82 to about 0.50 and increased regression RMSE by 5–6×.
  • Stress-tested dimensionality: TabDDPM accuracy and F1 fell about 20% from 32 to 128 dimensions, while TabSyn degraded about 30% from 16 to 128 dimensions.
  • Developed a training-dynamics membership-inference signal reaching TPR@0.1 FPR of 0.53 on Adult, 0.59 on Shopping, and 0.36 on Bank.

Applied differential privacy02

Differentially Private FinDiff

Adapted FinDiff into a differentially private tabular generator using Opacus and an RDP accountant, then swept privacy budgets on UCI Adult and audited both predictive utility and feature distributions.

0.32 → 0.00minority-class F1 after adding DP

Overall accuracy moved only from 0.79 to 0.76, while the downstream classifier collapsed into a majority-class predictor. The audit also identified category polarity reversal and artificial density at continuous-feature boundaries.
